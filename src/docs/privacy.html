Privacy Policy

aitji · Effective November 9, 2026 · Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information thatako.net and go.thatako.net collect, how it is used, and the choices you have, including when you connect your Google Drive account to upload files.

Terms of Service Contact

Who we are

thatako.net is a community infrastructure project built for students and teachers of Thatakophittayakom School (Thailand). It is operated by an individual student developer, aitji (Suriya Inchoo), who is the data controller for the information described here. The services covered are the URL shortener, the file sharing feature and the QR code generator at go.thatako.net, and the free subdomain service.

Information we collect

Link and file records

When you create a short link or a file link we store the slug you chose, the destination URL, a creation record, and a random secret key generated in your browser. The secret key is the only credential that lets you list, edit or delete your links. We do not ask for your name, email address or password.

Files uploaded to our server

If you choose server storage, the file contents, file names, sizes and MIME types are stored in Cloudflare R2 until the expiry date you selected (7 to 90 days) and then deleted automatically.

Files uploaded to your Google Drive

If you choose Google Drive, the file contents go from your browser directly to your own Google Drive and are never copied to our servers. We only store the Google Drive file ID, file name, size and MIME type so that the download page can be shown.

Technical data

Our infrastructure providers (Cloudflare and Vercel) process standard request data such as IP address, browser type and timestamps for security, abuse prevention, caching and aggregate analytics. We do not use this data for advertising.

Browser storage

Your secret key and theme preference are kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies.

Google user data

The optional Google Drive feature uses Google Sign-In (Google Identity Services) to request exactly one scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This scope only lets the app see and manage files and folders that the app itself created or that you opened with the app. It does not give us access to the other files in your Google Drive, and we do not request your contacts, calendar, email or any other Google data.

What we do with that access

  • Create (or reuse) a folder named go.thatako.net in your Drive.
  • Upload the files you select into that folder.
  • Set each uploaded file to "Anyone with the link can view" so the people you share the link with can download it.
  • Read the uploaded file's name, size, type and ownership so we can verify it and show the download page.
  • Read your display name, email address and storage quota, shown only to you in your own browser so you know which account is connected and whether you have enough space.

Access tokens

The Google access token stays in your browser's memory and expires after about one hour. When you finish an upload, the token is sent once, over HTTPS, to our server (go.thatako.net) so that it can confirm with Google that the files you listed exist, are not in the trash, and are owned by you. The server uses the token for that single check and discards it. We never store, log or reuse access tokens, and we never receive a refresh token. We do not store your Google email address, name or any Google profile data on our servers.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the file-upload feature you ask for, we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide that feature or to comply with law, we do not use it for advertising, and no humans read it.

How we use information

  • To redirect short links and serve file download pages.
  • To let you list, edit and delete the links created with your secret key.
  • To enforce storage limits and to prevent abuse, phishing and malware.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Sharing

Anyone who has a link can open the page and download the files behind it. Treat every link as public: do not upload files containing passwords, identity documents or other sensitive personal data. We share data with service providers only as needed to run the service (Cloudflare for hosting, storage and DNS; Vercel for the website; Google for Drive when you choose it), or if required by law or to protect against abuse.

Retention and deletion

  • Server files are deleted automatically when they expire. KV records follow shortly after.
  • Short links and Drive links remain until you delete them. Deleting a link in go.thatako.net removes our record immediately.
  • Google Drive files are yours. Deleting a link does not delete the file from your Drive; delete it in Drive if you want it gone. Moving the file to the trash or removing sharing stops the download page from working.
  • To revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have a record removed and you no longer have your secret key, email us and tell us the link.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Upload tokens are signed and short-lived, file downloads are served as attachments with content-sniffing disabled, and the download page runs under a strict Content Security Policy. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children and students

The service is intended for students and teachers of a secondary school. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is described above. Google accounts are subject to Google's own minimum age requirements.

Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and similar laws you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Because we do not collect names or emails, we may ask you to prove control of the relevant secret key or link before acting on a request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will be announced on thatako.net. Continuing to use the service means you accept the updated policy.
